Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Harttech is a digital studio and community based in Nairobi, Kenya. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we look after it.

What we collect

  • When you register as a member: your full name, email address, the area you are interested in, and the date you registered.
  • When you send us a message: your name, email address, subject and message.

We do not ask for passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information from visitors.

Why we collect it

  • To manage your membership and keep in touch about community activities.
  • To reply to your messages.

We only use your details for these purposes. We do not sell them or share them for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your details are stored securely with our database provider, Supabase. Only the Harttech administrator can view them. Visitors to the website cannot see any member or message details.

How long we keep it

We keep membership details while you remain a member, and messages for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry. After that they are deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To do this, email us at hello@harttech.com and we will respond as soon as we can.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The administrator login stores a sign-in token in the browser so the administrator stays logged in.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
